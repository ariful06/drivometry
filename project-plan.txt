## 3. Important improvements to make the app more useful

These requirements should be included in the plan Claude prepares.

| Area                    | Improvement                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Multiple vehicles       | Each fuel entry, part, expense, and maintenance record must belong to the correct vehicle.                                                                                 |
| Fuel calculations       | Use full-tank-to-full-tank consumption for reliable mileage; exclude or flag incomplete intervals rather than reporting misleading values.                                 |
| Odometer validation     | Detect duplicate, missing, or decreasing readings and let users correct mistakes.                                                                                          |
| Maintenance forecasting | Calculate next due date and kilometer reading independently; alert when either applicable threshold is reached.                                                            |
| Expense tracking        | Separate purchase/installation costs, routine servicing, repairs, and recurring ownership costs. Avoid double-counting a part cost and its associated maintenance invoice. |
| Flexible reminders      | Let users choose advance notice, repeat intervals, snooze, complete, and dismiss.                                                                                          |
| Offline reliability     | Store everything locally, schedule notifications locally, and require no login or backend.                                                                                 |
| Data portability        | Export to `.xlsx` or CSV and PDF; include date filters, vehicle filters, and all relevant record details.                                                                  |
| Data safety             | Provide database backup/restore, validation, and migration handling before releasing updates.                                                                              |
| Usability               | Quick-add fuel and expense actions, sensible defaults, searchable history, and accessible charts.                                                                          |

Key technical decision: A vehicle maintenance reminder may be triggered by time, distance, or whichever comes first. Distance-based reminders require an odometer update, so the app should explain when its distance estimate is stale. Operating-system restrictions also mean local notifications must be rescheduled and checked appropriately after reboot, app updates, or permission changes.

# Project: Offline-First Vehicle Fuel, Expense & Maintenance Manager

## Role

Act as a senior Flutter architect, product analyst, QA engineer, and GitHub project manager. Audit my existing application before proposing changes. Do not assume the project is new, and do not rewrite working features without evidence.

## Product objective

Develop an offline-first Flutter application for cars, motorcycles, and other vehicles. It tracks fuel consumption, vehicle ownership expenses, spare-part lifecycles, preventive maintenance, and local reminders. All core functionality must work without internet access, user accounts, or a backend.

## Functional requirements

### 1. Vehicle management and dashboard

* Support multiple vehicles, each with its own odometer, fuel history, parts, maintenance history, expenses, and reminders.

* Display recent activity, current-period expenses, fuel economy, and upcoming or overdue maintenance.

* Support configurable distance and currency units.

### 2. Fuel tracking

* Record date/time, odometer, volume, total price, unit price, fuel type, station, full/partial fill status, and notes.

* Calculate fuel economy using a documented full-tank-to-full-tank algorithm inspired by Fuelio.

* Handle partial fills, missing full-fill records, duplicate entries, odometer corrections, and invalid readings.

* Calculate fuel cost per distance unit, average fuel price, and period-based fuel expenses.

* Define calculation rules and unit tests before implementing changes. Do not invent precise consumption figures when the available data is insufficient.

### 3. Maintenance and parts

Create two tabs: Parts and Maintenance.

Parts:

* Store part name, category, compatible vehicle, purchase and installation dates, purchase/installation cost, expected lifespan, warranty, odometer at installation, and replacement history.

* Support lifespan in months, kilometers, or both.

* Track active, due, replaced, and retired parts.

* Preserve historical records when a part is replaced.

Maintenance:

* Include oil changes, filters, brakes, tires, batteries, servicing, repairs, and custom tasks.

* Support intervals such as 3, 6, 9, or 12 months and 1,000, 2,000, 5,000 km, or user-defined values.

* Store last-completed date and odometer, next due date and odometer, estimated cost, actual cost, notes, and service history.

* Support date-only, distance-only, and combined schedules.

* Define how next-due values are calculated and what happens when maintenance is completed early or late.

### 4. Expenses and statistics

* Categorize fuel, maintenance, parts, repairs, insurance, registration, taxes, parking, and miscellaneous expenses.

* Provide monthly, quarterly, yearly, and custom-range analytics.

* Include expense-distribution charts, monthly trend charts, category breakdowns, total cost, cost per kilometer, and vehicle comparisons.

* Prevent double-counting between maintenance invoices and parts purchased or installed as part of those invoices.

* Document financial aggregation rules and edge cases.

### 5. Notifications and reminders

* Provide a reminder center for upcoming, due, overdue, completed, and dismissed items.

* Support maintenance, part replacement, insurance, registration, inspection, and custom personal reminders.

* Allow advance notice, local notification scheduling, snoozing, completion, dismissal, and notification preferences.

* Handle date-based and odometer-based triggers, including stale odometer information.

* Work offline with local notifications. Handle permission denial, reboot, time-zone changes, app updates, and operating-system scheduling limitations where applicable.

* Avoid duplicate notifications and define how notification rescheduling works.

### 6. Export and data protection

* Export data to Excel-compatible XLSX/CSV and PDF.

* Support date-range and vehicle filters, meaningful column names, totals, and readable report formatting.

* Include local backup and restore, import validation, error handling, and database migration safety.

* Ensure export and restore work offline.

### 7. User experience

* Use bottom navigation for Home, Fuel, Maintenance, Statistics, and Reminders.

* Keep Parts and Maintenance as tabs within Maintenance.

* Provide quick-add actions, searchable history, sensible defaults, validation, empty states, and clear overdue indicators.

* Preserve existing design patterns where they are effective. Propose usability improvements without unnecessary redesign.
